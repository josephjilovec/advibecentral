# AdVibe Central

AdVibe Central is a concept-stage advertising entertainment and discovery platform built around one idea: **the advertisement is the content**.

## Product thesis

Most platforms make people tolerate ads. AdVibe Central is designed around advertising people may actively choose to watch, rate, save, discuss, compare, and share.

The platform is intended to combine:

- curated advertising discovery
- human-made, AI-made, and hybrid campaigns
- audience ratings and an AdVibe Score
- AI vs Human creative battles
- rankings and the AdVibe 100
- awards and category recognition
- profiles, watchlists, and saved creative
- future Vibe Points tied to meaningful platform participation rather than incentivized ad clicks
- future brand and agency submissions
- future creative intelligence and campaign-improvement services

## Current build

The current repository is a front-end concept build using plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It includes interactive category filtering, reactions, AI-vs-human voting, score sliders, modal flows, rankings, awards, and submission UI.

The campaign names and metrics in the concept UI are illustrative placeholders, not claims about real brands, campaigns, audience size, or measured performance.

## Direction

Long-term, the intended flywheel is:

**Great creative → audience → ratings/data → brands/agencies → better creative → larger audience**

A production implementation would require real authentication, database-backed accounts, moderation, media rights/licensing workflows, creator/agency attribution, anti-fraud controls, and clearly structured sponsored/reward activity.
