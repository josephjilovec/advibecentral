# AdVibe Central

AdVibe Central is a concept-stage advertising entertainment, discovery, rating, and creative-intelligence platform built around one idea: **the advertisement is the content**.

## Product thesis

Most platforms make people tolerate ads. AdVibe Central is designed around advertising people may actively choose to watch, rate, save, discuss, compare, and share.

The platform is intended to combine:

- curated advertising discovery and a broader searchable archive
- human-made, AI-made, and hybrid campaign disclosure
- audience ratings and an AdVibe Score
- AI vs Human creative battles
- rankings and the AdVibe 100
- awards and category recognition
- profiles, watchlists, and saved creative
- filters by creative source, mood, format, industry, region, and eventually campaign objective
- campaign files with creator/agency/brand credits and source links
- future attention/performance analytics with strict separation between community opinion and verified campaign metrics
- future Vibe Points tied to meaningful platform participation rather than incentivized ad clicks
- future brand and agency submissions
- future sponsored audience research
- future creative intelligence and campaign-improvement services

## Current front-end build

The current repository is a pre-deployment front-end concept using plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It now includes:

- animated advertising collage and high-density Creative Wall
- discovery/category filters plus keyword, source, mood, and duration filtering
- reactions and interactive sorting
- local watchlist behavior using browser storage
- campaign-detail modal with an illustrative attention curve and provenance labels
- AI-vs-human community voting
- interactive Vibe Score controls
- Vibe Pulse creative-trend concept
- AdVibe 100 leaderboard
- AdVibe Awards
- future rewards/Vibe Points UI
- brand and agency commercial lane
- submission and early-access modal flows
- responsive mobile layouts and reduced-motion handling
- favicon, web-app manifest, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and Vercel caching/security headers

The campaign names, audience counts, rankings, ratings, attention curves, and performance signals in the concept UI are illustrative placeholders, not claims about real brands, campaigns, audience size, or measured performance.

## Trust model

A production version should visibly distinguish:

1. editorial selection,
2. community opinion,
3. advertiser-supplied information, and
4. independently verified performance data.

AI-made and hybrid campaigns should be clearly disclosed. Campaign submissions should require appropriate authority to share the work and media.

## Direction

Long-term, the intended flywheel is:

**Great creative → audience → ratings/data → brands/agencies → better creative → larger audience**

A production implementation would require real authentication, database-backed accounts, moderation, media rights/licensing workflows, creator/agency attribution, anti-fraud controls, privacy/terms infrastructure, abuse reporting, and clearly structured sponsored/reward activity.
